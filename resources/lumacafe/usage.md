# Usage

## Ride the lift

1. Use **Call elevator** on the panel beside the doors. Use third eye if your server has a target provider or press **E** when the key prompt appears.
2. Wait for the car to arrive and the doors to open. The display above the doors shows its current floor and direction of travel.
3. Enter the cabin and use **Use lift screen**. In the close-up, move the mouse and click a floor. The **Open** and **Close** controls operate the doors while the car is stopped.
4. Leave the close-up with **Esc**, **Backspace** or right-click. Stay inside the cabin while the car travels. Riders move to the destination during a brief screen fade.

| Button | Floor |
|---|---|
| **G** | Café and bar with dining and hookah seating |
| **1** | Lounge with private dining and VIP seating |
| **2** | Event floor for private hire |
| **R** | Rooftop bar, pool and terrace |

A destination marked **Restricted** requires the job access configured by your server.
